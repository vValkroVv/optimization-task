## Goal

Determine whether a CUDA Graphs implementation improves repeated full training updates for the exact GIFT `gift_moe_3b_hybridep_fp8_compile.yaml` workload on one eight-H100 NVLink server, while preserving the configured MoE HybridEP FP8 computation, model and loss compilation, selective activation checkpointing, training state, and checkpoint behavior. Recommend a maintainable candidate only if the integrated evidence supports it; an evidence-backed no-benefit or incompatibility result is valid. A positive speedup is not a completion condition.

The campaign must cover forward, backward, and optimizer-update execution in the native training path. A compile flag, graph capture of forward alone, isolated operator timing, or successful replay of a fixture does not establish that the training update is captured or faster.
